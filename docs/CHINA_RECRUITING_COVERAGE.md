## Verified current openings

- Baidu / 百度: one official daily-internship AI Product role.
- RED / 小红书: four official internship roles across Product, AI Product and Growth.

All five pages displayed a job-specific application action during verification. None published an exact deadline, so none appears in **Closing soon**.

## Checked without a suitable current record

- ByteDance / 字节跳动: the official campus landing page describes current programmes, but no job-specific official URL was captured for this dataset.
- Tencent / 腾讯: official campus process information was captured, but no job-specific current opening was captured.
- Alibaba / 阿里巴巴: official campus portal checked; no job-specific current opening was captured.
- Meituan / 美团: official pages found through search did not expose a reliable current campus job detail suitable for ingestion.
- DJI / 大疆: official 2026 recruitment process is historical; it is retained only as labelled OA/interview evidence, not as an active opening.

## Missing company verification

The remaining tracked companies still require job-by-job official portal verification. They remain tracking targets only and contribute zero active opportunities. This includes JD, Huawei, Xiaomi, Pinduoduo, SHEIN, Li Auto, NIO, XPeng, DeepSeek, MiniMax, Moonshot AI, Zhipu AI, Dewu, Bilibili, NetEase, Kuaishou and the lower-priority original target directory entries.

## Stale or re-verification items

- All active opportunity records should be rechecked weekly because no public deadline was provided.
- DJI OA/interview evidence describes the archived 2026 cycle and must not be treated as current 2027 policy.
- Tencent publishes a general campus interview sequence; role-specific topics, round count and OA provider remain unknown.
