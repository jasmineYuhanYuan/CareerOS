# CareerOS data verification policy

## Admission rule

A record may be labelled verified only when its claims are supported by an official employer, university or government page. Community sources may support interview-experience summaries, but never official eligibility, salary, deadline, visa or vacancy-status claims.

## Required metadata

Every verified record contains:

- source and official URL
- source type
- verification flag
- last-updated date
- next-review date
- country, region and language
- confidence

Unknown facts remain `null` or “Not published”. They are never inferred from similar programmes or previous years.

## Review cadence

- Open opportunities: review at least monthly and before portfolio demonstrations.
- Closed or upcoming programmes: review when the employer announces the next intake.
- University programmes and fees: review quarterly and at each calendar-year change.
- Government visa material: review monthly and whenever legislation or department guidance changes.
- Community interview summaries: retain source dates and mark confidence no higher than Medium.

## Current coverage

As of 29 July 2026, the first verified slice contains:

- 9 programme-level early-career opportunity records from 4 official source pages
- 5 postgraduate programme records from 5 official university pages
- 4 organisation records connected to the verified opportunity sources

This is intentionally fewer than the requested 100 opportunities and 50
companies. The repository must not inflate the count with invented role
variants, inferred deadlines, guessed URLs or copied aggregator listings.

Interview, online-assessment, resume, salary and visa datasets remain pending.
Those subjects require their own source-by-source review; visa and salary
records in particular must not be added without current government or official
public evidence.
