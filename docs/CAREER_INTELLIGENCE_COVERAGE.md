## Known gaps

- The priority list of 20 Australian technology employers is not complete.
- The requested China employer list is only partially covered; the four included
  campus-recruitment portals have official employer sources, while current
  role-level openings and deadlines remain unverified.
- International universities are not yet included.
- Career calendars, company interview/OA records, standalone salary records and
  evidence-backed technology roadmaps remain unimplemented.
- Company office locations, tech stacks, visa policies and interview stages are
  left empty unless an official source explicitly supports them.
- Visa records are a source index and are not migration advice.
