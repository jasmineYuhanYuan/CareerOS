# Readiness score explanation

CareerOS scores are deterministic planning aids for one exact vacancy, programme or occupation pathway.

They measure stored evidence against sourced requirements. Confirmed evidence earns weight; unknown facts receive partial weight; missing or blocked requirements receive none. Required unknowns and blockers cap the score.

Every detailed score exposes:

- exact target;
- confidence;
- confirmed evidence count;
- unknown count;
- cap and cap reason;
- source evidence;
- missing and blocked facts.

The score does **not** estimate employability, admission probability, interview probability, offer probability or employer judgement. It changes when the profile, source record or requirement evidence changes. Sample records do not contribute to verified readiness.
